How much water does your family need for a week? What is in the cellar, and what is about to expire at the back of the pantry? Item90 maps your home room by room and tells you how long your supplies last and what to rotate.

What it does

Home map
Pantry, Cellar, Garage, Kitchen, Bathroom and Bedroom to start with. Rooms and containers can be added, edited and deleted — if a container holds supplies, the app warns you first.
Nearly 600 items
Each with its own icon, with fast search and a category filter. If something is missing, create your own item and pick one of 80 icons.
Needs calculator
Set how many people you are and how many days you want to last: bars show water, food and essentials (torch, first-aid kit, radio, blankets), using an indicative 3 litres of water and 2,100 kcal per person per day. Tap a bar to see which supplies make up the total.
Expiry radar
Lists supplies that are already expired or expire within 30 days, in date order, so you know what to rotate.
Minimum levels & shopping list
Set a minimum stock for an item: when it drops below, it joins the shopping list by itself. Add everything else by hand from the cart.
Print
The full inventory by room and container, or just the shopping list, on paper.
English / Italian
Two-language interface, with 8-bit sounds and effects you can turn off.
Works offline
No account, no ads, your data stays on the phone. The internet is only used to verify the license.

Privacy & verification

A personal reminder, not safety, medical or civil-protection advice. You enter quantities and dates yourself and they are not verified: always check the real label of food, medicine and water. Lists and needs are indicative. Data stays on your phone, unencrypted — do not store real passwords or codes.
The internet is only used to verify the license, at activation and occasionally afterwards when a connection is available: only the key and the technical request data are sent, never your supplies.
